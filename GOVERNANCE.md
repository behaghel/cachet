# Governance

## Policy changes

Changes to the [Policy Manifest](docs/POLICY_MANIFEST.yaml) -- the signed constitution that defines Trust Pack guardrails, crypto suites, and fairness rules -- require **14-day public notice** before taking effect.

This ensures that holders, relying parties, issuers, and auditors have time to review, comment, and adapt before policy changes affect live verifications.

## Decision-making

Cachet is currently maintained by a small core team. As the project matures, governance will evolve toward:

- **Policy Council**: manages Trust Pack definitions and rule changes
- **Oversight Council**: reviews appeals, manages incident response, publishes transparency reports
- **Independent auditors**: NGOs, consumer protection groups, and academic labs monitoring the transparency log

See [docs/VISION.md](docs/VISION.md) for the full governance vision and [docs/TRANSPARENCY_LOG.md](docs/TRANSPARENCY_LOG.md) for the audit architecture.

## Charter principles

From the Policy Manifest:

1. Consent is explicit, revocable, and logged
2. Minimum disclosure by default; predicates over attributes
3. Context-only outcomes (badges), no global scores
4. Deterministic policy; no ML gating
